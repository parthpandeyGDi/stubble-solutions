What this means in Punjab

Ownership and hiring are different choices.

The March 2025 scheme summary specifies CHC projects costing up to ₹30 lakh. Assistance is subject to eligible costs, machine-wise ceilings and the applicable sanction.

These are scheme provisions, not a live quotation or confirmation that an application window is open.

Rent-Free Machinery: Check What Is Covered

CAQM Direction 90, dated 9 May 2025, calls for planning rent-free CRM-machine availability for small and marginal farmers. A rent-free implement should not be presented as a guarantee of free tractor, fuel and labour.

Sources: CAQM direction summarised by the Government ↗

Before Booking or Buying

Confirm the current Punjab notification with the agriculture department: eligible model, subsidy ceiling, beneficiary category, application dates and sanction process. For hiring, ask for an itemised per-acre rate and a confirmed service date.

Implementation checklist, not an application guide. No current vendor prices have been assumed.

Fire Impact Versus Machine Distribution

GDi’s district comparison of new Super and Surface Seeders distributed in 2022–23 against changes in burnt area from 2021 to 2023 found a weak relationship. More equipment did not consistently coincide with less burning.

Sources: GDi distribution analysis, 2025 ↗

What the Comparison Can, and Cannot, Say

It identifies a delivery question, not a causal effect. A robust assessment must account for crop area, machine type, functionality, actual use and satellite measurement differences. Fire events and hectares burnt are not interchangeable.

Compare consistent geographies and seasons, and distinguish reported activity from verified outcomes.

03Choose the Right Package

Lower cost, with the
right agronomy.

Surface Seeder belongs in the affordability discussion. Balers belong in a separate collection-and-offtake discussion.

A lower-cost option to evaluate

Surface Seeder

PAU describes a cutter-cum-spreader with seed and fertiliser boxes, operated with a tractor of 40 hp or above. It broadcasts seed and fertiliser while cutting and spreading straw, followed by irrigation.

Assess levelling, soil moisture, residue cover and access to irrigation before choosing it. A machine demonstration is not enough to establish reliable performance on every field.

Sources: PAU Surface Seeder guidance ↗

Kapurthala field day / PAU, March 2024₹300Reported cost per acre

PAU’s Kapurthala field-day report cites this cost for Surface Seeder wheat sowing.

A demonstration-reported cost, not a current market tariff or a complete cost-of-cultivation estimate. Obtain local all-inclusive quotes before comparing options.

Sources: PAU field-day report ↗

The baler issue: a machine needs a market.

A baler shifts straw into an ex-situ chain. Plan collection, loading, transport, storage and a buyer together. An idle baler or an uncollected bale does not complete residue management. Separate baler allocations from in-situ sowing capacity in district plans.

CEEW’s Punjab interviews identified two operational bottlenecks: seven of eight baler owners struggled to obtain spare parts, while aggregators’ preference to dry straw for 3–5 days could conflict with farmers’ urgent field-clearance needs.

Sources: CRM support includes straw supply-chain machinery ↗ · CEEW operator interviews, 2025 ↗

Why a Service Bundle Matters

CEEW’s 2025 sample found that about 45% of renting CHCs provided a full implement, fuelled-tractor and driver package. The report uses roughly ₹2,000 per acre as a complete-service cost benchmark. Use this as a study benchmark, not a prescribed Punjab rate.

Sources: CEEW CHC study ↗

Learning from elsewhere

Service delivery can scale. Transfer the operating lesson carefully.

Bihar / CSISA, 2011–2014

17 → 1,624 Providers

CIMMYT reports that support with purchases, operator training and farmer awareness expanded a zero-till service-provider network, reaching around 44,700 acres in 2014.

Punjab lesson: develop operators and customers together.

This was zero-till wheat service expansion, not proof of equivalent no-burn outcomes in Punjab’s heavier paddy residue.

Sources: CIMMYT, 2015 ↗

Vaishali, Bihar / climate-smart villages

Pool Operations, Not Ownership

A CIMMYT case describes coordinating small neighbouring fields to improve the efficiency and timeliness of machinery operations.

Punjab lesson: test cluster bookings and route planning.

A service-design example. The same savings or adoption outcomes should not be assumed without a local pilot.

Sources: CIMMYT, 2013 ↗
