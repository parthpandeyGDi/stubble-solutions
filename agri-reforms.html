System pathway / Agricultural reforms

Reduce the pressure
before harvest.

Punjab can make residue management easier by widening the paddy-to-wheat window and reducing dependence on the paddy-wheat cycle where viable alternatives exist.

01Why Reform the Crop System

Burning pressure is partly designed into the calendar.

The Punjab Preservation of Subsoil Water Act delayed paddy transplanting to protect groundwater by aligning cultivation with the monsoon. The later calendar also pushes harvesting closer to wheat sowing, compressing the time available to manage straw.

The answer is not a single date change. It is a portfolio of staggered transplanting, short-duration varieties and viable non-paddy crops that protects water, farm income and the wheat window together.

02Sowing-Cycle Reform

Stagger the calendar without weakening water protection.

Different zones can plant in sequenced windows so that harvesting, mandi arrivals, machinery demand and residue management are not concentrated on the same days.

Phase 01

Western Canal Districts

Start with Muktsar, Fazilka, Faridkot, Ferozepur and Bathinda.

Phase 02

Northern and Kandi

Follow with Ropar, Hoshiarpur, Pathankot, Gurdaspur and SBS Nagar.

Phase 03

The district grouping above reflects Punjab's staggered schedule reported to the Rajya Sabha committee. Exact annual dates should remain responsive to monsoon, groundwater and crop conditions.

03Variety Strategy

Earlier maturity can create time.

Select a variety profile to see where it fits. Seed choice must balance duration, disease resistance, grain quality, yield and market demand.

Short-duration export rice

Pusa Basmati 1509

ICAR reports a maturity period of about 115 days, around 30 days earlier than Pusa Basmati 1121. Earlier harvest can widen the residue-management window, while the commercial case depends on Basmati quality and market demand.

AdvantageEarlier maturity and strong Basmati market
WatchQuality, price volatility and buyer access
UseTarget suitable Basmati-growing belts

Improved line of PB1509

Pusa Basmati 1847

An improved near-isogenic line of PB1509 with resistance to key diseases. It can retain the short-duration logic while reducing some production risk.

AdvantageDisease resistance plus PB1509 background
WatchSeed availability and quality segregation
UseDemonstrate with procurement partners

PAU short-duration rice

PR 126

PAU recommends this short-duration variety and specifies younger seedlings and a bounded transplanting window. It can help timely field vacation, but variety deployment must follow current PAU guidance.

AdvantageShort duration for the paddy-wheat system
WatchTransplanting age and date discipline
UseScale through certified seed and extension

04Crop Diversification

Farmers switch when the market switches with them.

Maize, cotton, pulses and horticulture can reduce paddy area in suitable zones. Agronomy alone will not drive adoption if procurement, price, processing and risk protection remain weaker than paddy.

Constraint

Alternative crops face uncertain buyers, quality grades and logistics.

Zone-specific crop choices, demonstrations and transition support.

Alternative produce has a dependable destination.

Paddy area shifts without transferring transition risk to farmers.

05Punjab Pathway

Test packages, not isolated interventions.

Combine seed, agronomy, procurement and farmer-risk protection.

  1. Model the calendar

    Set district windows using crop, water, mandi and fire data.

  2. Scale verified short-duration seed

    Link certified seed, demonstrations and buyer guidance.

  3. Build crop-market pilots

    Select zones with suitable agronomy and contract buyers before sowing.

  4. Protect transition income

    Reduce first-mover risk through price, insurance or outcome support.

  5. Measure system outcomes

    Track income, water, harvest timing, residue, fires and buyer performance together.
