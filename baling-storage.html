01Why Logistics Matters

The plant begins at the field, not at the factory gate.

Punjab must collect straw in a short harvest window and supply plants for much longer. That requires one coordinated chain across farmers, machinery, transport, depots and buyers.

05Punjab Pathway

Treat aggregation as infrastructure.

The objective is not more bales. It is dependable service for farmers and dependable fuel for plants.

  1. Start with contracted demand

    Translate plant requirements into catchment-level acreage, quality and monthly dispatch plans before harvest.

  2. Appoint lead aggregators

    Give accountable operators multi-season service areas with clear farmer, safety and delivery standards.

  3. Build shared depots

    Locate verified storage near residue clusters, with fire systems, access roads, weighing and insurance.

  4. Digitise inventory

    Track bale origin, moisture, weight, age, ownership and dispatch status from field to plant.

  5. Pay for performance

    Tie public support to timely field clearance and accepted plant deliveries, not machine ownership or bales produced.

Reference: Punjab State Action Plan for crop-residue management. Operational design should be validated against buyer contracts and district harvest calendars.
