In-situ / Biodecomposers

Help the straw break down.
Work within the crop calendar.

Biodecomposers are microbial formulations intended to accelerate crop-residue decomposition. Their value depends on field conditions, the application protocol and time available before the next crop.

01What is a biodecomposer?

A biological aid.
Not instant field clearance.

Pusa Decomposer, developed by ICAR-IARI, is a consortium of seven fungal species. It is intended to support the breakdown of crop residue under suitable conditions.

02Punjab usage and policy context

Application is recorded.
Impact needs its own evidence.

Keep three questions separate: where it was applied, what the action plan requires, and whether it improved outcomes on the treated field.

CAQM / Direction of 12 April 2024

Map each farm to a viable residue-management option.

CAQM called for farm-level mapping to in-situ machinery and biodecomposers, or ex-situ options, through state action plans.

This is a planning framework with multiple pathways. It should not be read as a requirement to apply biodecomposer on every Punjab field.

Sources: CAQM direction summarised in July 2024 ↗

03Where the approach is constrained

The field calendar
sets the test.

Biological performance alone is not enough. The package must also protect timely wheat establishment and make economic sense to the farmer.

01Available time+

Compare the expected treatment period with the actual interval between paddy harvest and wheat sowing. Do not assume the two will align.

02Field conditions+

Moisture, residue contact and the prescribed tillage sequence need to be managed. Spray coverage alone is not evidence of decomposition.

03Additional operations+

Include formulation, spraying, irrigation, machinery and labour in the cost comparison, along with any sowing delay.

04Local incremental benefit+

Results depend on the treatment and setting. Compare against the same field operations without the formulation, not only against burning.

Sources: ICAR technology description ↗ · Government application package ↗
