Move the straw.

01Why Ex-Situ

Some residue must leave the field to unlock value elsewhere.

In-situ methods retain straw on the farm. Ex-situ pathways create an alternative destination for residue that can be collected economically, helping clear fields while supplying low-carbon fuel, energy and materials.

Densified fuel route

Standardise residue for industrial combustion.

Pelletisation reduces handling volume and creates a more uniform fuel for thermal power plants, brick kilns and industrial systems. Viability depends on drying, densification cost, quality standards and enforceable demand.

Enabling layer

Baling and storage connect every ex-situ pathway.

Plants operate for much of the year, while paddy straw is generated in a short season. Aggregators must collect quickly, limit transport distance, manage fire and moisture risk, and release feedstock predictably.

At the fieldRaking, baling, loading and farmer coordination.

At the depotWeighing, quality checks, covered storage and fire safety.

At the plantScheduled deliveries, traceability and payment assurance.
